# Demo Knowledge Base

The public workflow uses the fictional demo company **BrightPath Digital**.

## Service scope

The company only confirms these services:

- SEO
- Google Ads
- Social Media Marketing
- Website Design
- Email Marketing

No package features, sub-services, methods, deliverables, or capabilities are defined beyond those names.

## Business information

**Business hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM UK time.

**Consultations:** Free 30-minute consultation for new potential clients.

**Support response time:** Normally within 1 business day.

**Refunds:** Reviewed individually. The chatbot must not approve or promise refunds.

**Cancellations:** Consultations can be cancelled or rescheduled using the booking link in the confirmation email.

**Payments:** Card payments and bank transfer.

**Human escalation:** Legal threats, fraud, payment disputes, account-security issues, serious complaints, and unusual compensation requests require human support.

**Privacy:** Customer information should only be used for providing the requested service and support.

**Location:** UK-based and able to work with clients remotely.
